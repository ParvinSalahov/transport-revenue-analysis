# Transportation Revenue Analysis in Azerbaijan

This project analyzes transportation revenue trends in Azerbaijan from 1995 to 2024 across different transportation sectors.
## Data Source

The dataset was obtained from the official Open Data Portal of Azerbaijan (Digital Azerbaijan).

Dataset: "Nəqliyyat sektorunda sərnişin daşınmasından əldə olunan gəlir, min manat"

Source:
https://data.gov.az

Publisher: State Statistical Committee of the Republic of Azerbaijan

Accessed: June 2026


## Project Overview

The analysis focuses on:
- Transportation revenue trends
- COVID-19 impact analysis
- Post-pandemic recovery comparison
- Market share analysis
- Correlation analysis
- ARIMA-based forecasting for air transportation revenues

## Tools and Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Prophet
- Statsmodels
- Jupyter Notebook

## Key Insights

- Railway transport experienced one of the largest revenue declines during the pandemic.
- Air transportation showed strong post-pandemic recovery.
- Road transportation had the highest share in total transportation revenue.
- ARIMA forecasting was used to predict air transportation revenue for 2025–2030.
